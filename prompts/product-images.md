# Smile Coffee - Product Image Generation Prompts

## Style Guide

- **Background**: Neutral cream (#F5F0E8) or soft warm tone
- **Lighting**: Soft studio lighting, natural feel, slight shadows
- **Angle**: 3/4 view, slightly elevated perspective
- **Style**: Minimalist, modern, clean, appetizing
- **Consistency**: All images should feel like part of the same collection
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1 (square)
- **Resolution**: 1024x1024

## Template

```
[Product name], professional food photography, 3/4 angle, soft studio lighting,
cream-colored background, minimalist style, appetizing, warm tones, high quality,
sharp focus, shallow depth of field
```
